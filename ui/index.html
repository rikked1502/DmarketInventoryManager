Connect your DMarket account

  1. On dmarket.com open Settings → Trading API and generate a key pair.
  2. Copy .env.example to .env in the app folder (next to app.py).
  3. Paste your public and secret keys into .env and save it.
  4. More accounts: add DMARKET_PUBLIC_KEY_ALT / DMARKET_SECRET_KEY_ALT (any name after the underscore).

Your keys stay on this computer. They are only read by the app to sign requests.
